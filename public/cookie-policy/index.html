Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies

Quick Summary

Domma uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. We respect your privacy and only use cookies with your consent (except for necessary cookies required for the site to function).

You have full control: You can accept all cookies, reject optional cookies, or customize your preferences at any time.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies allow websites to remember your preferences, track your browsing activity, and provide personalized content. Most modern websites use cookies, and they are essential for many online services.

How We Use Cookies

Domma uses cookies for three main purposes:

  • Essential Website Function: To make the website work properly and provide core features
  • Functionality: To remember your preferences and enhance your experience
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Necessary Cookies Required

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, authentication, and consent management. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
domma:cookie-consent Stores your cookie consent preferences 365 days
domma:auth_token Authenticates logged-in users Session / 7 days
domma:auth_user Stores basic user information for logged-in users Session / 7 days

2. Functional Cookies Optional

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They remember your choices (such as theme preferences) to provide a more personalized experience.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
domma:theme Remembers your chosen theme (light/dark mode) 365 days
domma:sidebar-collapsed Remembers sidebar state (expanded/collapsed) 365 days
domma:editor:* Auto-saves content in the Domma Editor Session

3. Analytics Cookies Optional

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve the website and user experience.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
domma:cookie-consent-accepted Flags whether analytics tracking is allowed 365 days
Anonymous page views Tracks anonymous page visits (IP addresses anonymised) 90 days (auto-deleted)

Privacy Protection

IP Anonymisation: All IP addresses collected through analytics are automatically anonymised. For IPv4 addresses, we mask the last octet (e.g., 192.168.1.100 becomes 192.168.1.0). For IPv6 addresses, we mask the last 80 bits.

Data Retention: Analytics data is automatically deleted after 90 days in compliance with GDPR.

Do Not Track: We respect the Do Not Track (DNT) browser setting. If DNT is enabled, we will not track your activity.

Third-Party Cookies

Domma does not use any third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The only third-party content loaded is:

  • Google Fonts: Used for typography (does not track users for advertising purposes)

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options to manage cookies:

1. Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit Domma, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can:

  • Accept All: Accept all cookies for the best experience
  • Reject All: Reject optional cookies (only necessary cookies will be used)
  • Customize: Choose which categories of cookies to accept

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

2. Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
  • View and delete individual cookies

Please note that blocking cookies may affect your ability to use some features of our website.

3. Do Not Track

Domma respects the Do Not Track (DNT) browser setting. If you enable DNT in your browser, we will not track your browsing activity or collect analytics data.
