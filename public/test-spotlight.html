Spotlight Effect Test

You should see a rotating white spotlight beam sweeping across this hero section.

The spotlight completes one full rotation every 25 seconds.

What to Look For:

  • A subtle white/light gradient beam rotating across the blue background
  • The beam should be semi-transparent (50% opacity)
  • Complete rotation takes 25 seconds
  • The text content should remain clearly readable on top

Troubleshooting:

  • Hard refresh: Press Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)
  • Check DevTools: Open Inspect Element and look at the .test-hero::before pseudo-element
  • Verify animation: The ::before should have transform rotating
  • Check stacking: z-index should be: ::before = 0, content = 1
