## Schema Builder

> Quick Start Guide:  **Add Fields:** Click or drag field types from the left panel into the canvas **Edit Properties:** Click any field card in the canvas to open the property editor **Configure:** Set field name, type, required status, labels, and validation rules **Test:** Preview your form in real-time at the bottom (if enabled) **Export:** Click "Export" to get JavaScript, JSON, or TypeScript code
