## Our mission

Domma was created to give developers a powerful yet lightweight alternative to heavy JavaScript frameworks. Building
modern web applications shouldn't need megabytes of dependencies or a complicated build.

The aim is to bring the best parts of familiar libraries - jQuery, Lodash and Moment.js - together into one coherent
package that is easy to learn and a joy to use.

## The team

Domma is developed and maintained by DCBW-IT, a team that enjoys making tools that keep web development quick,
accessible and enjoyable. We're committed to keeping Domma stable, well documented and steadily improving.

Domma is also what [Domma CMS](https://domma-cms.com) is built from - this site runs on it.
