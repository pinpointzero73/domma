## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Websites use them - and similar
browser storage such as localStorage - to work efficiently, remember preferences and tell site owners how the site is
used.

## How we use them

Domma uses cookies and browser storage for three purposes:

- **Essential site function:** to make the site work and provide core features
- **Functionality:** to remember your preferences
- **Analytics:** to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it

## What we use

### 1. Necessary - required

Essential for the site to work, such as remembering your consent choice. You cannot opt out of these.

[table striped="true"]
| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `domma:cookie-consent` | Stores your cookie consent preferences | 365 days |
[/table]

### 2. Functional - optional

Remember your choices, such as theme, for a more personal experience.

[table striped="true"]
| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `domma:theme` | Remembers your chosen theme (light or dark) | 365 days |
| `domma:sidebar-collapsed` | Remembers whether the sidebar is collapsed | 365 days |
[/table]

### 3. Analytics - optional

Help us understand how visitors use the site, collected anonymously.

[table striped="true"]
| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `domma:cookie-consent-accepted` | Records whether analytics is allowed | 365 days |
| Anonymous page views | Counts anonymous page visits (IP addresses anonymised) | 90 days, then deleted |
[/table]

### Privacy protection

- **IP anonymisation:** every IP address collected for analytics is anonymised. For IPv4 we mask the last octet
  (192.168.1.100 becomes 192.168.1.0); for IPv6, the last 80 bits.
- **Retention:** analytics data is deleted automatically after 90 days.
- **Do Not Track:** we honour the browser setting - with it on, we collect no analytics.

## Third parties

Domma uses no third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The only third-party content the site loads is:

- **Google Fonts:** the site's typeface (not used to track you for advertising)
- **jsDelivr:** a content delivery network serving a script library the site uses

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

## Managing your preferences

- **Your browser:** most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, clear them when you close
  the browser, or view and delete individual cookies. Blocking cookies may affect some features.
- **Do Not Track:** turn it on in your browser and we collect no analytics.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy for legal, regulatory or operational reasons. When we do, we update the date below,
and if the changes are significant we may ask you to consent again.
