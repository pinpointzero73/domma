## What data we collect

Domma collects limited data to understand how the framework is used and to improve the experience. The data we
collect includes:

- **Page views:** URL, page title, referrer, timestamp
- **Browser information:** user agent, screen resolution, viewport size
- **IP address:** automatically anonymised before storage (last octet masked for IPv4, last 80 bits for IPv6) for
  analytics and security purposes
- **User preferences:** theme selection, UI settings and other preferences stored in your browser's localStorage
- **Consent information:** your acceptance of this privacy policy and its timestamp

**We do not collect** personal information (name, email, address), payment information, passwords or any sensitive
personal data unless you explicitly provide it to us.

## How we use your data

Your data is used only for the following purposes:

- **Analytics:** understanding which pages and features are most popular
- **Service improvement:** finding bugs, improving performance and improving the documentation
- **Security:** preventing abuse, identifying malicious activity and protecting our infrastructure
- **User experience:** remembering your preferences (theme, settings) for a personalised experience

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in two places:

- **Our server:** analytics data (page views, browser information, anonymised IP addresses) is stored on our secure
  server and deleted automatically after 90 days, in line with GDPR storage limitation requirements.
- **Your browser (localStorage):** preferences (theme, settings, consent information) are stored in your browser's
  localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

## Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following data protection rights, which you can exercise
at any time by contacting us at [privacy@dcbw-it.co.uk](mailto:privacy@dcbw-it.co.uk). We respond within 30 days.

1. **Right of access (Art. 15):** to know what personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
2. **Right to data portability (Art. 20):** to receive a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used,
   machine-readable format.
3. **Right to erasure (Art. 17):** to have your personal data deleted from our systems.
4. **Right to object:** to the processing of your personal data for analytics. You can also:
   - enable **Do Not Track** in your browser - we honour it and collect no analytics;
   - clear your browser's localStorage to remove stored preferences and consent information;
   - use your browser's private browsing mode.
5. **Right to rectification:** to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
6. **Right to restriction of processing:** to ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances.

### Additional privacy protections

Beyond what GDPR requires, Domma follows these privacy-first practices:

- **IP anonymisation:** every IP address is anonymised before storage (Art. 5(1)(c) - data minimisation)
- **90-day auto-deletion:** analytics data is deleted automatically after 90 days (Art. 5(1)(e) - storage limitation)
- **Do Not Track:** we honour the browser setting
- **No third-party tracking:** we do not share your data with advertisers or third-party trackers
- **Minimal collection:** we collect only what is needed to improve the service

## Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

- all data is sent over HTTPS
- analytics data is stored on secure, access-controlled servers
- we review and update our security practices regularly
- we do not store sensitive information that could identify you personally
